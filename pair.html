Pair Your Device

Link your WhatsApp account below, then reveal your SESSION_ID once pairing is complete.

Example: Tanzania number 0712 345 678 becomes 255712345678.

----

No phone number needed — just scan the QR code with WhatsApp.

How to link

  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device.
  3. Choose Link with phone number and enter the code above, or scan the QR code.
  4. Keep this page open — the status panel will update automatically.

Waiting to start

Generate a pairing code or QR code on the left to begin. You need to pair your device first before a Session ID can be created for you.

Never share this Session ID with anyone — it gives full access to your WhatsApp account. It has also been sent to your WhatsApp and removed from this server.
